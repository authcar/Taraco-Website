ABOUT TARACO

We started as a small design-and-build crew, and grew only because the work held up after we left.

We still keep our own ceiling, flooring, wall and electrical teams in-house — the people who draw the plan are the ones accountable for the wall it becomes. We're not interested in a look that photographs well and lives badly. A room has to survive a family, a rainy season and a few years of ordinary life before we call it finished.
